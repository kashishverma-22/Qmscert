Certification Process

From application to certification.

A transparent and structured certification journey designed to keep your organization informed, prepared, and confident at every stage.

01

Application & Scope

Submit an application for your locations to define the certification scope, audit days, and receive a formal Proposal & Agreement.

02

Stage One Audit

Documentation review and evaluation of your management system's implementation level, concluding with a detailed written report.

03

Stage Two Audit

Comprehensive assessment of operational processes, sampling objective evidence and agreeing on a Corrective Action Plan (CAP) if needed.

04

Certificate Issuance

Within 15 days of CAP approval and fees, your official Certificate of Registration is issued with international validity.
