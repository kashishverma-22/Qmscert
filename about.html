Who We Are

Your Global Partner for Certification & Compliance

QMS Global is an international certification body specializing in management systems and sector-specific assessment and certification. Since 1996, we have built long-term relationships with clients across business sectors worldwide.

We focus on client priorities, minimize bureaucracy, and provide practical, cost-effective solutions. Through our registered GlobalCert™ mark, we provide testing, analysis, and evaluation to determine conformity with recognized international standards.
