CERTIFICATION JOURNEY

Our Certification Process

Follow a structured journey from application to successful certification with professional support at every stage.

01
STEP 01

Application

Begin your certification journey by submitting your organization details, scope of certification and applicable requirements.

  • Organization details
  • Certification scope
  • Applicable standard
02
STEP 02

Application Review

Our certification team reviews the submitted information to understand your organization and determine the certification requirements.

  • Application review
  • Scope verification
  • Certification planning
03
STEP 03

Stage 1 Assessment

The management system documentation and organizational readiness are reviewed to determine preparedness for the next stage of certification.

  • Documentation review
  • System readiness
  • Assessment planning
04
STEP 04

Stage 2 Assessment

The implementation and effectiveness of the management system are assessed against the requirements of the applicable ISO standard.

  • Implementation review
  • Process assessment
  • Compliance evaluation
05
STEP 05

Corrective Actions

Where applicable, identified findings are addressed through appropriate corrective actions and reviewed by the certification team.

  • Findings identification
  • Corrective action
  • Review and closure
06
STEP 06

Certification Decision

After successful completion of the certification requirements, the certification decision is completed and the certificate is issued.

  • Final review
  • Certification decision
  • Certificate issuance
07
STEP 07

Surveillance

Periodic surveillance activities help verify that the certified management system continues to meet the applicable requirements.

  • Periodic assessment
  • System monitoring
  • Continual improvement
08
STEP 08

Recertification

At the end of the certification cycle, the management system can undergo recertification assessment to continue certification.

  • Recertification assessment
  • System effectiveness
  • Continued certification
